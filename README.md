# Your sportswear product website

This is a ready-to-publish, single-page sportswear product showcase. It works on phones and desktop computers and has no checkout system: visitors use your WhatsApp link to order.

## Change it for your business

Open `index.html` in any text editor and replace:

1. **NORTHLINE** with your business name (there are two locations). This is a fictional placeholder brand.
2. Each product name, description, and `$` price with your own.
3. Each image link inside `src="..."` with a link to your product photo. A simple way: upload a photo to a free image host or use your own image file in this folder and change it to `src="your-photo.jpg"`.
4. `15551234567` with your WhatsApp number in international form, with no `+`, spaces, or punctuation. Example: a US number `+1 555 123 4567` becomes `15551234567`.
5. `hello@kindredgoods.example` with your email address.

## View it on your computer

Double-click `index.html`. It will open in your web browser.

## Publish it free with GitHub Pages

1. Make a free account at [github.com]().
2. Create a new repository named `my-shop` and choose **Public**.
3. Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js` from this folder. Do not upload the README unless you want to.
4. In the repository, open **Settings → Pages**. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, then select `main` and `/ (root)`. Save.
5. Wait about one minute. GitHub will display your free website link, similar to `https://your-name.github.io/my-shop/`.

You can change the website anytime by uploading the updated files again. GitHub will republish it automatically.
